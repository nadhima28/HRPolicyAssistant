Leave Policy 2026

Vacation
- Full-time employees get 15 vacation days per year.
- Part-time employees get 8 vacation days per year.
- Contractors are not eligible for paid vacation.
- Unused vacation days can carry over to next year, up to 5 days.
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

Sick Leave
- Full-time employees get 10 sick days per year.
- Part-time employees get 5 sick days per year.
- A doctor's note is required for sick leave longer than 3 days in a row.

Parental Leave
- Full-time employees get 12 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

Holidays
- The company observes 10 paid holidays per year.
- If a holiday falls on a weekend, it is observed on the nearest weekday.

Bereavement Leave
- Employees get up to 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member.
